# Knowledge evolution

The research exploration graph also retains New technique → potential use →
existing problem → attempt → new understanding. Problem difficulty need not be
known in advance. Exploratory leads preserve attributable goal/structure/proof-role
connections and a next action; they may later develop into transfer hypotheses
and bridge propositions with validation plans. Attempt maturity does not alter
the problem's derived scientific status. See [technical exploration](TRANSFERS.md).

An event log records problem posing, bottleneck discovery, partial progress,
mechanism introduction, scoped resolution, challenges, reopening and supersession.
Events are append-only; a correction references a prior event rather than editing
it. Scientific date and discovery time are separate and replayable.

Resolution compares the original Scope against Result.scope. Exact matching
formulations with attributable reviewed evidence may support full resolution.
Extra assumptions imply at most conditional resolution. Changed metrics/models
are incomparable without explicit proof. Missing attribution or unknown implication
remains unverified; a paper's claim alone cannot close an open problem.

ProblemEvolutionTimeline derives current status using qualified assessments.
Challenges/reopening remain visible. Supersession explicitly references previous
events. Paper revision invalidates dependent comparisons/resolutions/breakthrough
assessments for re-review without deleting earlier evidence or events.

Breakthrough workflow retrieves previous results/attempts, bottlenecks and mechanisms,
checks new scope, and records the exact technical delta. It retrieves other open
problems sharing a bottleneck or explicit mathematical structure. Transfer proposals
remain hypotheses, with missing proof obligations and a first falsification test.
